# AI disclosure

**LoRA Studio was made with help from AI, alongside many friends (humans!).** AI tools helped write and review parts of the code and the documentation.

**A human is behind it.** I'm coco, and I decide what goes into LoRA Studio and review every change before it's merged. If you have a question or a concern, ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/cococolacake/lora-studio/discussions) and I'll answer it myself.

**It's free, forever.** LoRA Studio is open source under the [MIT license](https://github.com/cococolacake/lora-studio/blob/main/LICENSE) and will always be free to use, with no accounts, subscriptions or paid tiers.

This page is about how LoRA Studio was *made*. The training itself is done by [kohya-ss/sd-scripts](https://github.com/kohya-ss/sd-scripts) on your own computer.
